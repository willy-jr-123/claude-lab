August 18, 2026

US-Iran ceasefire MOU expires as Trump rules out extension, threatens Oman

The 60-day memorandum of understanding between Washington and Tehran lapsed without a follow-on deal, and President Trump told reporters Iran is unlikely to accept the terms he wants, calling instead for its surrender. Trump also threatened to bomb Oman if it "gets in the way" of US efforts to reopen the Strait of Hormuz, even as Tehran and Muscat near an agreement on managing shipping through the strait. A senior Iranian official told Reuters that Iran will shift to a "fully offensive" military posture, saying Tehran plans a "timely and precise" attack to break the stalemate, while Trump maintained there are "no talks or conversations going on, or scheduled" with Iran.

Source: CBS News, Al Jazeera

Kushner-Netanyahu marathon session ends without firm Gaza ceasefire commitment

White House envoy Jared Kushner met Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu in Jerusalem in a lengthy session aimed at reviving the stalled Gaza ceasefire plan, days after Netanyahu rejected the Trump-backed 15-point roadmap. Kushner voiced optimism about the plan's prospects afterward, but the talks ended without a firm Israeli commitment to the latest US proposal, leaving the core disputes — the pace of any Israeli withdrawal and Hamas's disarmament — unresolved.

Source: NPR

Ukraine hits Moscow with one of its largest drone barrages as Russian strike kills 10 in Kharkiv region

Ukraine launched more than 600 drones toward the Moscow region overnight, among its largest such attacks of the war, just two days after a similarly heavy salvo. Russia responded with a strike using small, jet-powered Banderol cruise missiles on Pechenigy, a village roughly 40 kilometers from the Russian border, killing at least 10 people in a residential area. President Volodymyr Zelensky called the attack "brutal" and pledged a response, as Russia's cumulative combat losses since February 2022 approached 1.47 million personnel by Kyiv's count.

Source: Al Jazeera, Euronews

Canada and US race to strike trade deal hours before 50% tariffs take effect

Prime Minister Mark Carney spoke by phone with President Trump on Monday as Canadian negotiators Dominic LeBlanc and Janice Charette continued talks with US Trade Representative Jamieson Greer and Commerce Secretary Howard Lutnick, seeking to head off 50% US tariffs on roughly $20 billion of Canadian goods due at the August 19 deadline. Carney described the negotiations as "very intense and delicate," while LeBlanc said afterward that the "job is not yet done." Trump has invoked Section 338 of the Tariff Act of 1930 to impose the duties, covering goods from alcohol and dairy to vehicles, lumber and electronics, unless a deal is reached.

Source: BNN Bloomberg, Bloomberg

Russia ships drone parts and explosives to Iran via Caspian Sea, bypassing Hormuz blockade

Moscow is sending drone components, ammunition and TNT to Iran across the Caspian Sea, according to a European government document obtained by NBC News, helping Tehran replenish stockpiles depleted by US and Israeli strikes during the war. US officials estimate Iran lost roughly 60% of its drone arsenal in the fighting, and the Caspian route lets Russia resupply Tehran without transiting the blockaded Strait of Hormuz. The shipments underscore deepening Moscow-Tehran military cooperation as both countries face separate conflicts with the United States and its allies.

Source: NBC News, The Times of Israel

US military focus on Iran war leaves Indo-Pacific allies exposed as China presses territorial claims

Analysts warn that the prolonged Iran war continues pulling US munitions, ships and attention away from the Asia-Pacific, with depleted stockpiles expected to take years to rebuild even after the fighting ends. Defense officials in the region say China has grown more assertive while Washington is preoccupied, including confrontations between Chinese coast guard vessels and Philippine forces near contested reefs in the South China Sea. Analysts note the diversion raises longer-term concerns about US readiness to deter a potential crisis over Taiwan.

Source: Stars and Stripes
