Xi Jinping arrives in Washington for state visit as Trump summit looms over AI, trade and Taiwan

Chinese President Xi Jinping and his wife Peng Liyuan arrived in Washington for a three-day state visit, the first Chinese state visit to the capital since 2015, ahead of a September 24 summit with President Trump. Trade, tariffs, Taiwan, Iran and the global race for AI supremacy are expected to dominate the agenda; analysts say a new communication channel on AI-related national-security incidents is the most concrete outcome likely, while chip export controls are reportedly not on the formal agenda despite Beijing's interest in relaxing them.

Source: CNBC, Bloomberg

Iran's Pezeshkian tells UN "we will never bow our head" in defiant rebuke of Trump

Iranian President Masoud Pezeshkian addressed the UN General Assembly a day after Trump said he was weighing whether to "annihilate" Iran, holding up photos of children he said were killed by US and Israeli strikes and accusing Washington and Israel of fueling global instability. Pezeshkian said Iran was open to "dialogue and diplomacy" but would not negotiate under threat of force, and separately voiced support for the Palestinian cause; a US delegate walked out during the speech.

Source: CNBC, CNN

Zelensky offers Russia an "energy ceasefire" and meets Trump as Moscow threatens winter strikes

Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky said Kyiv is ready for a reciprocal ceasefire on energy-infrastructure strikes if Russia halts attacks on Ukraine's power grid, and called for a trilateral meeting with Trump and Putin. Meeting Trump on the sidelines of the UN General Assembly, Zelensky said the US president signaled openness to licensing Patriot air-defense interceptor production in Ukraine, even as Russia's defense ministry said it was preparing "massive strikes" on Ukrainian energy infrastructure ahead of winter.

Source: CNBC, Kyiv Post

Gaza ceasefire remains fragile as Board of Peace briefs UN and strikes continue

The Trump-led Board of Peace, tasked with overseeing Gaza's ceasefire and reconstruction, gave a high-level briefing on the sidelines of the UN General Assembly as the plan remains stalled, with Hamas resisting disarmament and Israel continuing near-daily strikes. Monitors reported Israeli drone strikes, shelling and home demolitions across Gaza overnight, including an attack that killed three Palestinians in the Tuffah neighborhood of Gaza City, underscoring the gap between the nominal truce and conditions on the ground.

Source: IMEMC, CNN

Trump meets Venezuela's Rodriguez for first time since Maduro's capture

Trump held a pull-aside meeting with Venezuelan acting President Delcy Rodriguez at a UN General Assembly reception in New York, their first encounter since US forces captured former president Nicolas Maduro in a January raid. Rodriguez's delegation, which includes Venezuela's oil minister and officials from state oil company PDVSA, has separately been holding talks with US officials and multilateral lenders on energy, mining and debt; she is due to address the General Assembly on Wednesday.

Source: U.S. News, i24NEWS

US and 14 Latin American nations sign "Shield of the Americas" hemispheric security declaration

The United States and 14 governments across Latin America and the Caribbean, including Argentina, Colombia, Peru and Ecuador, issued a joint declaration under the Shield of the Americas coalition, pledging closer coordination on economic, security and multilateral issues. The framework, established by Trump in March 2026 as a counter-cartel coalition, involves participating states sharing intelligence and coordinating operations against organized crime networks; the September 22 declaration marked the coalition's second major gathering.

Source: U.S. Department of State, Rio Times
