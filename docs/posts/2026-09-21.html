Trump threatens to "wipe out" Iran's economy or leadership as Iran warns of "painful" retaliation

President Trump told Fox News the only options left for Iran were a deal, letting its economy "rot," or "wiping Iran out," ratcheting up pressure ahead of Iranian President Masoud Pezeshkian's arrival at the UN General Assembly this week, where Trump has said he would be open to meeting him. Iran's military said its intelligence indicated the US and its allies were preparing a new large-scale strike and vowed a "painful" response across the Middle East if one comes, deepening a stalemate that has dragged on for months despite a battered Iranian economy and a global energy-price spike.

Source: CNBC, The Jakarta Post

Merz's CDU suffers historic wipeout in German state election as AfD surges to 38%

Chancellor Friedrich Merz called the results a "disaster" after his Christian Democratic Union won just 4.9% of the vote in Mecklenburg-Vorpommern, risking the party's first-ever failure to win a seat in a German state legislature. The far-right Alternative for Germany more than doubled its vote share to 38%, though it remains locked out of government by the mainstream parties' "firewall" policy; the Social Democrats held second place at 35% and are expected to form a coalition with the Greens and the Left party.

Source: CNN, CNBC

North Korea fires two ballistic missiles toward East Sea, its first launch in over a week

South Korea's military said North Korea launched two short-range ballistic missiles from the Wonsan area, with the first flying about 450 kilometers and the second more than 600 kilometers before landing in the sea. Both are believed to be from the Hwasong-11 series and mark Pyongyang's first ballistic missile test in eight days. South Korea's presidential office called for an immediate halt to the launches, which violate UN Security Council resolutions, and convened an emergency national security meeting.

Source: ABC News, The Korea Herald

Israeli strikes kill four Palestinians in Gaza, including an 11-year-old boy, despite ceasefire

An 11-year-old boy was shot dead by Israeli forces in the al-Mawasi "safe zone" in southern Gaza, while a 31-year-old woman was shot in the head by an Israeli tank near the Bureij refugee camp, among at least four Palestinians killed in attacks across the enclave. The deaths underscore how, nearly a year after the October 2025 ceasefire, disputes over implementing its second phase have left the situation in Gaza oscillating between fragile calm and recurring violence, with no lasting improvement in humanitarian conditions.

Source: Al Jazeera

Ukraine's "Operation Vivaldi" reclaims 125 sq km in Donetsk as Russian drones hit Sumy and Kyiv regions

Ukraine's 3rd Army Corps said its four-phase counteroffensive north of Lyman has now recovered a total of 125 square kilometers of territory in northern Donetsk Oblast, including several liberated villages, disrupting Russia's push to encircle Ukraine's Donetsk defensive belt. Overnight, Russian drone strikes killed two people and injured three, including a 14-year-old girl, in the Sumy region, while attacks on the Kyiv region damaged 34 civilian sites across the Bucha, Boryspil and Fastiv districts.

Source: Kyiv Independent, Mezha

China issues 11-day ultimatum on Trump-Xi summit over Taiwan arms sale

Beijing has warned Washington to abandon a roughly $14 billion Taiwan arms package, already authorized by Congress but not yet formally notified by the Trump administration, or see the September 24 Trump-Xi summit called off. Xi Jinping has told Trump the Taiwan issue could push the two countries into "conflict" if mishandled, while Trump is reportedly weighing delaying a decision on the sale to preserve the meeting, which his administration has cast as a centerpiece of this autumn's diplomacy.

Source: Vision Times, The Diplomat
