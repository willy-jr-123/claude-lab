US House votes for a third time to end Trump's Iran war, but veto looms

The House passed a war powers resolution late Tuesday by a vote of 220-204 that would direct President Trump to end military action against Iran absent explicit congressional authorization, with seven Republicans joining nearly all Democrats in favor — three of them backing such a measure for the first time. It is the third time the House has approved a version of the resolution, but none has reached Trump's desk, and the White House has signaled he would veto it if it did.

Source: Al Jazeera

Von der Leyen proposes Canada as EU's first "associate member" amid Trump trade rift

European Commission President Ursula von der Leyen used her State of the Union address in Strasbourg on Wednesday to invite Canada to become the bloc's first "associate member," an unprecedented status whose details remain undefined, as Prime Minister Mark Carney looked on. The overture follows Carney's push in recent days for a "unique alliance" with Brussels short of full membership, as Trump's tariffs and repeated talk of annexation push Ottawa to diversify away from Washington. The two sides envision cooperation spanning manufacturing, AI, critical minerals and energy.

Source: Al Jazeera, NPR

Kosovo's ex-President Thaci convicted of war crimes, sentenced to 25 years

The Kosovo Specialist Chambers in The Hague convicted former President Hashim Thaci of murder, torture, cruel treatment and arbitrary detention committed as a top commander of the Kosovo Liberation Army during the 1998-99 war for independence from Serbia, sentencing him to 25 years in prison. Judges found him responsible for the murder of 96 political opponents and suspected collaborators, the illegal detention of 385 people and the torture of 303 more. Thaci, 58, denied all charges, and the verdict has proved deeply divisive in Kosovo, where many view the KLA's wartime leadership as national heroes.

Source: Al Jazeera, NPR

Saudi-backed Yemeni forces intensify strikes on Houthis ahead of expected ground offensive

Yemeni government forces backed by Saudi Arabia stepped up air and artillery strikes this week on Houthi positions in Mokha, Dhubab and Khokha — territory the Iran-aligned rebels seized in recent weeks near the strategic Bab al-Mandeb Strait. Military officials described the campaign as preparation for a larger ground offensive to retake the area. The escalation comes as tensions between the Houthis and Saudi Arabia remain elevated following a disputed drone incident near Mecca, which Riyadh called a "red line" and the Houthis denied responsibility for.

Source: Washington Times

Russian drone kills five on passenger bus in southern Ukraine

A Russian drone struck a scheduled intercity minibus on an open road in the Nikopol district of Ukraine's Dnipropetrovsk region early Wednesday, killing all five people aboard and wounding at least seven more, according to regional military administration head Oleksandr Hanzha. President Volodymyr Zelenskyy called the strike an "atrocity" with "no military rationale," linking it to Russia's broader campaign against logistics and infrastructure near the front line. It is the latest in a series of Russian strikes on civilian transport in the region.

Source: Al Jazeera

Satellite imagery shows surge in construction at Iran's underground Pickaxe Mountain site

A new analysis by the Center for Strategic and International Studies, based on six years of satellite imagery, found more construction activity in 2026 at Iran's deeply buried Pickaxe Mountain complex near Natanz than at any prior point in the site's history. Recent work — hardening tunnel entrances, paving internal roads and removing excavation spoil — suggests the project has shifted from excavation toward construction inside the mountain, which analysts believe is intended as a hardened haven for Iran's nuclear program. The imagery could not confirm or refute earlier reports that centrifuge components had already been moved into the complex.

Source: CSIS, CNN
