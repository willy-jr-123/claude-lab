August 26, 2026

Trump says US Navy has cleared all mines from Strait of Hormuz as Rubio signals shift to sanctions

President Trump said the US Navy has informed him that all Iranian anti-ship mines have been removed or detonated from the Strait of Hormuz, calling it a watershed moment in the nearly six-month war and warning Tehran that any vessel laying new mines will be "immediately and systematically destroyed." Separately, Secretary of State Marco Rubio told several allied foreign ministers that Washington does not expect to launch a new round of major strikes on Iran "for the time being," with the campaign shifting toward economic pressure, though he left open the option of retaliating if Iran attacks first. Iran said it has resumed talks with Oman over managing the strait despite the mounting sanctions pressure.

Source: Axios, Axios

Canada and US trade war grinds on despite talk of a possible pullback

Canada is preparing to impose retaliatory tariffs of up to 50% on roughly $27.6 billion of US goods starting September 8, matching Washington "dollar for dollar" after trade talks collapsed and the US imposed steep tariffs on Canadian goods and autos. Prime Minister Mark Carney has suspended negotiations and likened the dispute to a conflict, saying "we got attacked," while analysts note both sides could still step back from an all-out trade war, though it remains unclear whether they will.

Source: Washington Post

Ukrainian drone strikes kill three in Russia as Moscow pounds Ukraine with missiles and drones

Russia fired an Iskander-M ballistic missile, a Kh-31P guided missile and around 150 drones at Ukraine overnight, with strikes recorded at 17 locations and Ukraine's Kherson combined heat and power plant suffering severe damage; air defenses downed or suppressed the bulk of the drones. Ukraine struck back with a wave of drone attacks across Russia that killed at least three people and set fires at the Lukoil refinery in Kstovo and at a Wildberries e-commerce warehouse, part of Kyiv's sustained campaign against Russian energy and logistics infrastructure. Russia's defense ministry said it intercepted more than 400 Ukrainian drones overnight, including over occupied Ukrainian territory.

Source: Al Jazeera

Gaza envoy accuses Israel of violating disarmament plan as Netanyahu rejects required withdrawals

Nickolay Mladenov, the Board of Peace's Gaza envoy, said Israel's continued strikes in the enclave violate the US-backed disarmament roadmap, even as Hamas has committed to halting military activity, with unresolved issues over weapons, tunnels and convoys giving ammunition to those who want to resume the war. Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu has come out against the plan, arguing it will not achieve Hamas's disarmament, and said Israel will not carry out the phased troop withdrawals the roadmap requires, deepening doubts about the fragile ceasefire's durability.

Source: Times of Israel

Iran plotted to assassinate Netanyahu's son in Florida, report says

Iran planned to assassinate Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu's eldest son, Yair, while he was living in the Miami area, according to a US law enforcement source cited in multiple Israeli outlets. Israeli intelligence learned of the plot last December and alerted US authorities, prompting Yair Netanyahu to leave Florida abruptly and return to Israel; Iranian operatives were reportedly monitoring his residence and movements. The elder Netanyahu confirmed this week that Iran had "tried to murder" one of his sons, without initially naming which one.

Source: Times of Israel, Jerusalem Post

Dozens killed in rebel attacks on Sudan's Kordofan region

A medical organization said the Sudan People's Liberation Movement-North, a rebel group allied with the paramilitary Rapid Support Forces, killed 27 people including four children in attacks in South Kordofan, triggering a large wave of displacement toward Abu Hitan and Kadugli. The violence comes as the broader battlefield in Sudan continues to shift, with the army recapturing towns in North Kordofan and the RSF facing a wave of defections even as it retains pockets of strength elsewhere, underscoring fears that the conflict is fragmenting rather than ending.

Source: Al Jazeera
