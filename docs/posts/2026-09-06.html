Witkoff and Kushner arrive in Kyiv after Moscow talks with Putin end without a breakthrough

US envoys Steve Witkoff and Jared Kushner landed in Kyiv on Sunday to brief President Volodymyr Zelensky, a day after more than three hours of talks with President Vladimir Putin in Moscow produced no announced breakthrough. Kremlin aide Yuri Ushakov described the meeting as "constructive, frank and useful" without detailing specific outcomes, while Putin reportedly told the envoys he has "full trust" in US mediation. The two sides agreed Putin and President Trump would stay in touch, and Witkoff and Kushner said they would carry Putin's assessments into their Kyiv talks, as a temporary pause in strikes on Kyiv tied to the visit held.

Source: NPR, Al Jazeera

US disables three Iranian oil tankers after Iran fires on Navy warships; Tehran vows "more painful" response

US forces permanently disabled two Iranian crude carriers, the M/T Downy near Kharg Island and the M/T Stark 1 near Jask, and destroyed a third tanker, the M/T Kylo, in the Gulf of Oman, after Iran's Revolutionary Guard fired missiles at a US aircraft carrier and a guided-missile destroyer without causing damage or injuries. Washington said the tankers were part of a shadow fleet funding the Revolutionary Guard, while Iran's IRGC claimed on Sunday that it separately struck a US drone boat attempting to enter the Strait of Hormuz, a claim the US called a "total lie." Iranian Parliament Speaker Mohammad Bagher Ghalibaf warned that further attacks on Iran would "meet a faster, heavier and more painful response."

Source: NBC News, Al Jazeera

Eight Muslim-majority nations warn Israel's Gaza expulsion plans threaten Trump's peace effort

The foreign ministries of Saudi Arabia, Jordan, the UAE, Turkey, Qatar, Egypt, Indonesia and Pakistan issued a joint statement Sunday warning that Israeli officials' proposals to force Palestinians out of Gaza "flagrantly violate" international humanitarian law and threaten regional stability. The statement followed remarks from Israeli Defense Minister Israel Katz that the government was prepared to remove Palestinians pending US approval, and from far-right National Security Minister Itamar Ben Gvir, who outlined removing 250,000 Gazans in the plan's first year. The eight nations said such measures directly undermine President Trump's Gaza peace plan, which they noted includes a rejection of forced displacement.

Source: Arab News

Israeli strike kills two Palestinians, including a child, in Gaza City

An Israeli strike hit a vehicle in western Gaza City on Sunday, killing two people including a young girl and wounding six others, according to the director of Al Shifa Hospital. The strike came as Israel's military faces mounting international scrutiny over its Gaza operations, with IDF Chief of Staff Lt. Gen. Eyal Zamir describing the country as being in a "tense and volatile period across all sectors." Separately, Israeli forces reported finding a rocket during a raid in a West Bank village, and the military said it would investigate a report that troops dropped a Palestinian man from a moving vehicle.

Source: Haaretz, The Times of Israel

Yemeni government forces claim gains against Houthis as battle for Taiz-Mokha corridor intensifies

Fighting between Yemen's Saudi-backed government forces and Iran-aligned Houthi rebels continued for a third day around Taiz and the Red Sea port city of Mokha, with Houthi fighters attempting to sever the road linking the two and firing more than a dozen ballistic missiles at rural areas in Taiz and Hodeidah. Yemeni military sources reported dozens of Houthi fighters killed alongside government casualties in the clashes, which follow the collapse of a 2022 ceasefire and mark some of the heaviest fighting in years along a corridor near the strategic Bab al-Mandab Strait. Analysts are divided on how durable the government's recent battlefield gains will prove.
