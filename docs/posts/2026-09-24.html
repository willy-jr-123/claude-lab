September 24, 2026

Trump hosts Xi at the White House as the two sides extend their trade truce into 2027

Chinese President Xi Jinping was received at the White House on Thursday for the main day of his three-day Washington state visit, the first by a Chinese leader to the US capital since 2015. Trump and Xi opened with remarks on artificial intelligence before a Rose Garden military review and a bilateral meeting expected to cover trade, Taiwan and the Iran war; ahead of the summit the two sides agreed to extend their one-year trade truce, previously set to expire November 10, by two months to January 10, 2027.

Source: CNN, CNBC

Russian ballistic missiles kill two in Kyiv as strikes hit across Ukraine overnight

Russian ballistic missiles struck Kyiv overnight into Thursday, killing two people and injuring at least eight, with falling debris partially destroying a private house in the capital's Dniprovskyi district and trapping residents under rubble, according to Mayor Vitali Klitschko. The attack was part of a wider wave of Russian strikes that killed at least eight people across Ukraine, coming a day after both Kyiv and Moscow vowed at the UN to continue their long-range strike campaigns.

Source: Kyiv Independent, Euronews

Venezuela's Machado blocked from returning home for a third time in 24 hours

Opposition leader and Nobel Peace Prize laureate María Corina Machado was turned back three separate times within a single day this week as she tried to travel from her exile in Panama back to Venezuela, by air and by sea. It is at least her seventh unsuccessful attempt to return since June, a pattern that has continued even as acting President Delcy Rodríguez's delegation holds talks with US officials in New York on energy, mining and debt.

Source: The Hill, Rio Times

Netanyahu and Abbas both take the UN stage as Gaza ceasefire strain persists

Palestinian Authority President Mahmoud Abbas addressed the UN General Assembly by pre-recorded video after being denied a US visa, warning against "another Nakba" and urging faster implementation of the stalled Gaza peace plan, including a halt to Israel's planned E1 West Bank settlement. Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu spoke later the same day, expected to focus heavily on Iran's nuclear and missile programs a month before Israel's elections, in a rare trip that did not include a meeting with Trump or other US officials.

Source: The National, Al Jazeera

Argentina's Milei calls UN a "useless organization" in fiery General Assembly speech

Argentine President Javier Milei told the UN General Assembly the body's "sacred covenant has been broken," accusing it of allowing "chaos, violence and international terrorism" to flourish and branding it a "useless organization" run by "arrogant parasites." Milei, one of Trump's closest Latin American allies, underlined Argentina's alignment with the US and Israel, accused the UN of systematically voting against Israel, and said the organization was "looking the other way" on Argentina's Falkland Islands claim.

Source: Al Jazeera, UN News

Guterres set to meet Lavrov on UN sidelines as General Assembly week continues

UN Secretary-General António Guterres was scheduled to hold a bilateral meeting with Russian Foreign Minister Sergei Lavrov on Thursday evening on the margins of the General Assembly's high-level week, according to the Secretary-General's office. The meeting comes as wars in Ukraine, Gaza, Iran, Sudan and Myanmar dominate this year's session, with Lavrov set to deliver Russia's own address to the Assembly on Saturday.

Source: TASS, UN Secretary-General
