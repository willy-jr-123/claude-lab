August 22, 2026

Canada-US trade talks collapse, 50% tariffs on Canadian goods take effect

Negotiations between Washington and Ottawa broke down Friday night just before a midnight deadline, and a new round of 50% US tariffs on roughly $20 billion of Canadian goods took effect early Saturday. Prime Minister Mark Carney suspended talks minutes before the deadline and said Canada would "match those tariffs dollar for dollar." US Trade Representative Jamieson Greer said Canada had "declined to finalize the trade deal under the terms agreed earlier this week," while Ottawa countered that last-minute US changes to previously agreed terms were unfair and undermined confidence in any deal. No further talks are currently scheduled.

Source: CP24 (CTV News), CNBC

Russia strikes Kyiv and its main airport hub for a second straight day ahead of Independence Day

Russian ballistic missiles hit Kyiv and the Boryspil airport area for the second consecutive day, part of an intensifying bombardment as Ukraine approaches its August 24 Independence Day. Ukrainian officials say dwindling Patriot interceptor supplies are leaving the capital increasingly exposed to ballistic strikes that current air defenses cannot reliably stop. Separately, Russia's air force reported a large overnight drone-and-missile barrage across multiple regions, with dozens of drones intercepted but a number of impacts still recorded.

Source: Euromaidan Press

US readies "toughest sanctions in history" on Iran as Trump calls Hormuz "American territory"

The Trump administration is preparing to unveil a major new sanctions package targeting Iran and entities that trade with it on Monday, with officials describing it as among the most sweeping economic isolation campaigns ever assembled. President Trump used a campaign rally to declare the Strait of Hormuz "American territory," while traffic through the waterway remains at roughly 20% of pre-war levels. A senior official close to Iran's supreme leader said Tehran "will not submit" to the pressure campaign, even as Iranian state media indicated Iraqi oil tankers would be permitted to transit the strait.

Source: CNN, Fox News

Israeli drone strike in southern Syria wounds civilian; Damascus calls it a sovereignty violation

The Israeli military struck a vehicle in the town of Beit Jinn in southwestern Syria on Saturday, saying it targeted a militant in the final stages of planning an imminent attack on Israeli troops. Syrian state media reported that a civilian was wounded in the strike, and Syria's Foreign Ministry condemned the attack on what it called a civilian vehicle, calling it a "flagrant violation" of Syrian sovereignty. The strike is the latest in a series of Israeli operations inside Syrian territory near the border.

Source: The Times of Israel, Xinhua

Israeli strikes kill at least 18 in Gaza as ceasefire disarmament standoff continues

Israeli strikes across Gaza killed at least 18 people, according to local health officials, as an Israeli minister said there was no agreement in place to halt the attacks. The strikes come amid a stalemate over the ceasefire's next phase, with mediators pressing Hamas to disarm before reconstruction and an international stabilization force can move forward, and Israel continuing military operations it says target militants despite the truce.

Source: Al-Monitor, Haaretz

North Korea fires ballistic missile barrage as Trump signals openness to new Kim summit

North Korea launched around 10 short-range ballistic missiles toward its eastern waters this week, days after President Trump said he would meet again with Kim Jong Un later this year and ordered a scaled-back US-South Korea Ulchi Freedom Shield exercise. Seoul's military condemned the launches as a violation of UN Security Council resolutions, while analysts said the barrage was meant to reinforce Pyongyang's recent warnings that its stance is not mere rhetoric even as Washington explores renewed diplomacy.

Source: U.S. News, CBS News
