Yemen edges toward renewed war as Houthi-government clashes intensify

Fighting between Iran-backed Houthi rebels and Yemeni government forces raged overnight in the mountainous province of Taiz, with at least one soldier killed and another critically wounded. It is part of a broader escalation over the past month, including deadly Houthi drone and missile strikes on government-held areas and the west coast port of al-Makha. U.N. special envoy Hans Grundberg told the Security Council that Yemen faces a greater risk of returning to large-scale conflict than at any point since a 2022 truce ended nearly a decade of civil war.

Source: Al Jazeera

Israel strikes Hamas commander in Gaza, breaking week-long lull

The Israeli military said it killed a Hamas commander, identified by local sources as Muhannad Saada, in a drone strike on Beit Lahiya in northern Gaza. The IDF said the target was planning an attack on Israeli forces. It was the first targeted strike Israel has announced since August 3, and it comes under closer scrutiny given a US-backed "Board of Peace" plan pushing for the disarmament of Gaza and a halt to such strikes as part of a broader ceasefire effort.

Source: The Times of Israel, Haaretz

Manila accuses Beijing of "extortion" over threats to Filipino workers

Philippine officials told a Senate hearing that the Chinese Embassy warned it would move against Filipino workers in China unless Manila released Chinese nationals detained in a May raid on a steel plant in Misamis Oriental over alleged immigration, labor and environmental violations. Sixty-nine Chinese nationals were arrested in that raid. Philippine Defense Secretary Gilberto Teodoro called a related reported crackdown on about 100 Filipinos in Hong Kong and Macau "plain and simple extortion and blackmail."

Source: Inquirer.net, Philippine News Agency

Iran's foreign minister accuses France and allies of human rights "hypocrisy"

Iranian Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi lashed out at France and other Western governments after they issued a joint statement, backed by 32 countries and the EU's foreign policy chief, condemning Iran's use of capital punishment against foreign-linked rioters. Araghchi said Western support for Israel's actions in Gaza and its involvement in the recent Iran conflict had destroyed any moral standing to "lecture the world" on rights, while France's foreign ministry separately accused Iran of "bloodshed" over its use of the death penalty.

Source: Middle East Eye, Euronews

Russian bombardment of Ukrainian cities kills 13, wounds around 90

Russia intensified its overnight bombardment campaign against Ukrainian cities this week, killing at least 13 people and wounding roughly 90 others in a 24-hour period, according to Ukrainian officials. The strikes come as Moscow says it remains open to resuming peace talks on the basis of the earlier Istanbul framework, while fighting and drone strikes continue unabated along the front line and in the country's interior.

Source: Euronews

Taliban detain two UN staff in Herat, drawing rights groups' concern

Taliban intelligence officials detained two Afghan employees of the U.N. political mission in the western city of Herat, with the U.N. saying it has not been told of any charges or allowed to visit the men. The detentions come amid a worsening humanitarian crisis in Afghanistan and cross-border fighting between Taliban and Pakistani forces. Human Rights Watch warned the move "seriously threatens" the U.N.'s ability to deliver aid across the country.

Source: The Washington Post
