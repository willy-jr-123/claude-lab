Sweden's centre-left wins narrow majority, Andersson launches coalition talks

Final results confirmed Magdalena Andersson's Social Democrat-led bloc secured a three-seat majority in the 349-seat Riksdag over outgoing Prime Minister Ulf Kristersson's right-wing alliance, positioning Andersson to return to power after losing it in 2022. She has invited the Left Party, Centre Party and Greens to a joint meeting to begin forming a government, though disagreements over cabinet seats, taxation, nuclear power and labour policy could draw the process out for weeks, as it did after the 2018 and 2022 elections.

Source: Euronews, ABC News

Carney tells EU Parliament Canada and Europe "aren't fair-weather allies," rebukes Trump tariffs

Canadian Prime Minister Mark Carney addressed the European Parliament in Strasbourg, welcoming European Commission President Ursula von der Leyen's proposal to make Canada the bloc's first "associate member." Without naming President Trump, Carney warned that "economic integration is now being weaponized — tariffs being used to exert pressure," and said deeper EU-Canada cooperation on critical minerals, defence, AI and energy would bolster collective resilience without compromising Canadian sovereignty. He said Canada's Parliament would debate and vote on any future arrangement.

Source: CBC News, Euronews

Von der Leyen's EU security plan takes shape: NATO-style "Emergency Security Protocol"

Details continued to emerge from Ursula von der Leyen's State of the Union address proposing an "Emergency Security Protocol" modelled on NATO's Article 4, under which any of the EU's 27 member states facing a serious hybrid threat — sabotage, cyberattacks, arson or drone incursions — could trigger coordinated consultations among all members. She also proposed a new "European Security Council" that would include partners such as Canada, Norway, Ukraine and the UK, part of a broader push for European self-reliance following a string of suspected Russian-linked hybrid incidents.

Source: Euronews, Breaking Defense

Trump says he's "hopeful" Iran war is nearing an end as Saudi-Houthi fighting escalates

President Trump told reporters he hoped the nearly seven-month war against Iran was approaching an end, saying Iran "wants to make a deal" and that he had heard from Tehran directly. The comments came even as fighting intensified elsewhere in the region, with Saudi aircraft striking Houthi positions in Yemen and Houthi fighters firing drones and missiles at Saudi cities. A US-Iran memorandum signed in June had set a 60-day window for a final agreement that expired in mid-August without one being reached.

Source: CNBC, US News

UNICEF: renewed Yemen fighting pushes over 12 million children deeper into crisis

UNICEF warned that two weeks of intensified fighting between Saudi-backed Yemeni government forces and Houthi rebels has displaced more than 104,000 people, including over 57,000 children, disrupting access to health care, water and education. Since September 3, at least nine children have been confirmed killed and 12 injured, with three more missing after an attack on the coastal road to Aden; the UN human rights office separately verified at least 40 civilian casualties in the same period, with women and children accounting for half.

Source: UNICEF

War-damaged Gaza building collapses, killing at least 20 sheltering inside

A six-storey residential building in western Gaza City that had previously been struck and left structurally unsound collapsed, killing at least 20 Palestinians and trapping close to 100 more, many of them displaced families sheltering inside. Rescue crews dug through rubble largely by hand, lacking the heavy equipment needed to reach survivors. The Israeli military denied striking the building during the war. An estimated 2,000 buildings across Gaza are considered at risk of collapse, with many residents still living in them for lack of alternatives.

Source: Al Jazeera, The National
