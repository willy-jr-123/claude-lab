Trump delays 50% Canada tariffs at last minute after tentative trade deal

Less than two hours before 50% US tariffs on roughly $20 billion of Canadian goods were due to take effect, President Trump said he was pausing the duties for three days after Ottawa and Washington reached a tentative agreement. Prime Minister Mark Carney warned that "important work" remains to finalize the broader deal, with outstanding issues including Canada's retaliatory auto tariffs, provincial restrictions on American alcohol sales, and dairy quota arrangements. Trump had invoked Section 338 of the Tariff Act of 1930 in July to impose the tariffs on USMCA-compliant goods.

Source: NPR, Bloomberg

UAE suspends all trade with Iran after accusing Tehran of renewed missile attack

The United Arab Emirates halted all trade, commercial exchanges and financial transactions with Iran indefinitely after accusing Tehran of firing two ballistic missiles toward Gulf shipping routes, one of which reportedly landed inside UAE territorial waters. The missile fire triggered nationwide shelter warnings in the UAE, the first such alert in weeks. Iran's foreign ministry rejected the accusation as "baseless," with a spokesman suggesting it was a "false flag operation." Before the war, the UAE was among Iran's largest trading partners, supplying more than 30% of its imports.

Source: Al Jazeera, The Washington Post

Israeli strike kills about 10 at Gaza police post, deepening doubts over ceasefire

An Israeli strike on a police station in Gaza City killed roughly ten people, according to local hospitals and the Palestinian Red Crescent, with Hamas's Interior Ministry saying the dead included eight police officers and a 13-year-old girl. The Israeli military said it was targeting Hamas commanders, including one it said had helped plan the October 7, 2023 attack. The strikes came just days after US envoy Jared Kushner met Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu to press for restraint and revive the stalled Gaza ceasefire roadmap, casting fresh doubt on its prospects.

Source: Arab News, AP via ClickOnDetroit

Trump reaffirms no talks with Iran as Hormuz blockade stays "in full force"

President Trump said there are "no talks or conversations going on, or scheduled" with Iran, a day after suggesting a back channel had opened with Iran's Revolutionary Guard Corps. He said the US naval blockade of Iranian ports remains "in full force and effect," though other maritime traffic remains free to pass, and posted a map depicting the Strait of Hormuz as US territory. Trump has separately threatened to bomb Oman if it "gets in the way" of a deal governing Hormuz shipping, after the 60-day US-Iran ceasefire memorandum lapsed without a follow-on agreement.

Source: Al Jazeera, NBC News

Russia pounds Ukraine with ballistic missiles and drones overnight

Russian forces launched two Iskander-M ballistic missiles and 65 Shahed-type and decoy drones at Ukraine overnight, with Ukrainian air defenses reporting 58 intercepted or jammed over the country's north and east. The drones were launched from Russia's Kursk region and occupied areas of Donetsk and Crimea. The barrage followed a heavy daytime assault a day earlier, in which Russia fired 76 jet-powered Shahed-type drones at Ukraine, underscoring the continued intensity of aerial exchanges even as both sides trade blame over stalled peace efforts.

Source: Ukrainska Pravda

Sudan war displaces 200,000 more as fighting and flooding intensify

The UN's International Organization for Migration reported that intensifying fighting between the Sudanese Armed Forces and the Rapid Support Forces has displaced roughly 200,000 more people since late 2025, compounding a crisis worsened by seasonal floods. The war, now in its fourth year, has driven a total of 8.6 million people from their homes. The UN also noted that humanitarian access is being further strained by disruptions to global supply chains through the Red Sea and Strait of Hormuz, linked to the broader regional conflicts.

Source: UN News
