September 22, 2026

Trump defends Iran war and repeats "eight wars ended" claim in UN General Assembly address

Addressing the UN General Assembly's high-level week, President Trump defended his administration's military campaign against Iran, arguing US strikes prevented Tehran from building a nuclear weapon and accusing it of seeking to erect a missile arsenal ahead of a "mad dash" to a bomb. He repeated his claim to have ended eight wars or conflicts during his presidency, a characterization independent fact-checkers and AP have disputed, and touted First Lady Melania Trump's efforts to reunite children separated by Russia's war in Ukraine.

Source: CNN, NBC News

UK agrees to refuel Saudi jets defending against Houthi drone and missile attacks

British Prime Minister Andy Burnham said the UK would provide the Royal Air Force's air-to-air refueling support to Saudi Arabia, helping Saudi jets intercept Houthi drones and missiles after a weekend attack targeted Riyadh for the first time in years. The mission is limited to defensive support rather than strikes on Houthi positions and is expected to last only weeks, officials said, as the Iran-aligned group presses a broader offensive to seize Yemen's entire Red Sea coast, including the strategic Bab al-Mandab strait.

Source: The Washington Post, Al Jazeera

Russian strikes kill at least eight and wound dozens across Ukraine, hit Dnipro industrial plant

Russia launched more than 200 drones and several dozen missiles overnight, killing at least eight people and wounding 37 across Ukraine, Ukrainian officials said. A ballistic missile strike on the Interpipe industrial plant in Dnipro killed four employees and halted production, while a partially destroyed residential building in Kyiv's Dniprovskyi district injured four more; further strikes hit Kryvyi Rih, Pavlohrad and towns in the Kharkiv and Donetsk regions. The barrage came as Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky prepared for talks with Trump.

Source: Kyiv Independent, U.S. News

North Korea's Kim hails "new-type weapon" days after missile launch draws condemnation

State media reported that Kim Jong Un oversaw the test of a "new-type weapon" system, presenting it as evidence of the country's advancing military technology. The announcement came two days after South Korea and Japan condemned North Korea's launch of two short-range ballistic missiles off its east coast as a threat to regional security, and follows Pyongyang's rejection of a recent IAEA resolution demanding it halt nuclear activities, which it says are "irreversibly and permanently fixed" by domestic law.

Source: CP24 (AP), Euronews

Gaza ceasefire strains further as Israeli forces accused of new violations, Hamas denies counter-claims

Local monitors reported roughly a dozen Israeli ceasefire violations in Gaza overnight, including airstrikes, shelling and home demolitions, with low-flying drones over Gaza City and attacks near displacement sites. Hamas rejected separate Israeli claims that its fighters had breached the truce, calling them false and accusing Israel of using them to justify further escalation. Gaza's Health Ministry has recorded more than 1,300 people killed since the October 2025 ceasefire took effect, underscoring how the year-old truce continues to oscillate between fragile calm and violence.

Source: IMEMC, Al Jazeera

Trump and Xi head into Washington summit with Taiwan, trade and Iran looming over talks

Trump is set to host Xi Jinping in Washington on September 24 for their third meeting since Trump returned to office, with trade, tariffs, Taiwan, AI competition and China's Iranian oil purchases expected to dominate. Washington has been pressing Beijing to cut its imports of Iranian crude to tighten economic pressure on Tehran, while Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent said it is "incumbent upon China" to help find a solution; officials on both sides say no major breakthrough is expected, with the summit instead aimed at steadying fragile ties.

Source: ABC News, CNBC
