Trump-Xi summit wraps up heavy on pageantry, light on substance

The Washington summit between President Trump and Chinese leader Xi Jinping concluded with a state dinner after two days dominated by ceremony rather than major deliverables. Xi told Trump he hopes the US will oppose Taiwan independence, while both leaders stressed the need to "manage" artificial intelligence development; beyond the previously announced two-month extension of their trade truce, analysts said the cautious summit produced few substantive agreements on trade, Taiwan or Iran.

Source: Bloomberg, CNBC

Netanyahu calls Gaza genocide claims the "biggest lie of the century" as delegates walk out

Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu told the UN General Assembly that allegations of genocide in Gaza were "the biggest lie of the century," defending Israel's war against Iran as "one of the easiest decisions" he has made and branding walkout delegates "moral cowards." His address triggered mass walkouts after the Palestinian Mission urged member states to leave the chamber, leaving much of the hall empty by the time he finished speaking.

Source: NPR, UN News

Iran offers seven-day plan to reopen Strait of Hormuz and restart nuclear talks

Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi said Iran has given US negotiators a new proposal, modeled on the June Islamabad memorandum, to reopen the Strait of Hormuz within seven days in exchange for a full ceasefire including Lebanon, release of roughly $12 billion in frozen Iranian assets, and waivers on US oil sanctions. The offer follows a three-hour discussion between US and Iranian officials through mediators at the UN, though the Trump administration has previously rejected returning to the June agreement's terms.

Source: Al Jazeera, Washington Post

France to send troops and air defenses to protect Saudi Arabia's Yanbu oil terminal

President Emmanuel Macron said France will deploy soldiers, radars and defense systems to protect Saudi Arabia's Red Sea port of Yanbu, the terminus of the East-West Pipeline that lets the kingdom export crude while the Iran war has largely closed the Strait of Hormuz. The move follows repeated Houthi missile and drone attacks and suspected strikes from Iran-backed militias in Iraq on Saudi oil infrastructure; Macron said the deployment aims to stabilize oil markets and stressed France is "not getting involved in any conflict."

Source: Euronews, Bloomberg

UAE suspends Iranian airline flights over US sanctions; Iran bans UAE overflights

The UAE's General Civil Aviation Authority suspended flights by Iranian carriers to and from the country until further notice, citing US Treasury sanctions imposed this month on 27 airlines and related entities supporting Iran's aviation sector. The UAE joins Oman, Iraq, Azerbaijan, Georgia and Turkmenistan in restricting Iranian airlines, and Iran retaliated by banning UAE flights from its airspace.

Source: Al Jazeera, The National

Zelensky backs Turkey as venue for trilateral summit with Russia

Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky said Kyiv is "very open" to a Turkey-hosted leaders' summit with Russia after President Erdogan offered to organize one during talks on the sidelines of the UN General Assembly in New York. Zelensky said he also discussed ceasefire efforts and reopening the grain corridor with officials from India, the UAE and Egypt, though the Kremlin said this week that conditions are not yet ready for high-level talks with Ukraine.

Source: Daily Sabah, Axios
