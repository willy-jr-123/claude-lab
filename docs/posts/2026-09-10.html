IAEA board refers Iran to UN Security Council for first time in 20 years

The International Atomic Energy Agency's 35-nation board of governors passed a resolution reporting Iran to the UN Security Council, with 23 votes in favor, three against and eight abstentions; Russia, China and Niger voted no. The move follows up on a June 2025 finding that Iran was in non-compliance with its non-proliferation obligations, centered on unexplained uranium traces found at undeclared sites and a continuing standoff over inspector access to facilities bombed by Israel and the US last year. Because Russia and China hold Security Council vetoes, the referral is unlikely to trigger new UN action, but it marks a symbolic diplomatic escalation against Tehran.

Source: Al Jazeera

NATO allies say they foiled Russian submarine plot to sabotage Arctic undersea cables

Reuters reported that NATO allies caught Russian submarines this spring training to deploy a covert weapon designed to sever critical undersea cables near the Svalbard archipelago without leaving a trace. The operation was reportedly run by Russia's GUGI undersea warfare directorate using deep-sea submersibles, and a joint British, Norwegian and American effort tracked and confronted the vessels before they could complete the exercise. Britain and Norway had already disclosed a related covert Russian operation in Arctic waters in April, and Norway is now accelerating plans for a new fiber-optic cable linking Svalbard to the mainland by 2028.

Source: Defense News (Reuters)

Ukraine's deepest strike yet hits Russian gas plant 3,200 km from the border

Ukraine's Special Operations Forces struck the Novy Urengoy gas condensate treatment plant in Russia's Yamalo-Nenets region on September 9, roughly 3,200 kilometers from the Ukrainian border — the farthest Ukrainian strike of the war and the first to reach that region. The attack broke the previous distance record set by a July strike on the Omsk oil refinery. Russian officials confirmed drones reached the area and caused a fire, which they attributed to falling debris, and reported no casualties.

Source: The Kyiv Independent

Russian drone strike on Sumy shopping mall kills two, injures 20

A Russian drone struck the Manufactura shopping center near the center of Sumy in northern Ukraine, killing two 21-year-olds and injuring 20 others, including three children, according to regional governor Oleh Hryhorov. The strike sparked a fire and heavily damaged the building. It is the latest in a series of Russian strikes on the city, which sits close to the Russian border and has faced repeated attacks throughout the war.

Source: The Kyiv Independent, The Globe and Mail

Israeli strike kills four, including two children, in northern Gaza

An Israeli strike on the northern Gaza community of Beit Lahiya killed at least four people, including two young sisters, according to officials at Shifa Hospital, where the bodies were taken. The dead included a husband and wife along with their two daughters, aged 8 and 12. The Israeli military said the strike "eliminated" a militant involved in attacks on its forces, one of a series of strikes it says are carried out in response to threats under the fragile ceasefire in place since October.

Source: AP via WTOP

Trump says Iran war will end "immediately" after US midterm elections

President Trump told reporters he expects the war with Iran to end "immediately after the election because they can't hold out any longer," suggesting Tehran is trying to prolong the conflict and keep fuel prices elevated to hurt Republican prospects in the November midterms. The comments came as fighting continued in the Strait of Hormuz, with Iranian attacks reported to have damaged US military aircraft at a base in Jordan days earlier. Trump also predicted gas prices would fall once the conflict concludes.
