August 29, 2026

Russian drone strike on ammunition warehouse near Kyiv kills at least 37

A Russian drone attack struck a warehouse in the village of Myla in Kyiv region's Bucha district, triggering a large fire and a series of secondary explosions that authorities said were caused by ammunition stored at the site. At least 37 people were killed and dozens more wounded, with more than 50 nearby residential buildings damaged as the blaze spread to homes and a restaurant. The strike came amid a stretch of near-constant Russian aerial attacks on the Kyiv region.

Source: Al Jazeera, CNN

Iran's IRGC Navy declares "full control" of Strait of Hormuz, rejects US claims it is open

The navy of Iran's Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps said Saturday that its control over the Strait of Hormuz is "complete and decisive," dismissing US statements that the waterway is open to commercial shipping as "an obvious lie." It said vessels would not be allowed to transit without prior coordination with Iranian authorities until the US ends its military campaign and fulfills outstanding obligations tied to the six-month-old war. The White House maintains the strait remains open under an active US naval blockade, leaving the rival claims over the critical oil chokepoint unresolved.

Source: Al Jazeera, Arab News

IDF says it killed a Hamas commander in strike near Gaza hospital

The Israeli military said it struck and killed a Hamas "military wing commander" it accused of planning attacks, in the Deir al-Balah area of central Gaza near a hospital. The strike is the latest in a series of Israeli operations against Hamas and Islamic Jihad figures in Gaza in recent days, which Israel has continued despite US calls to scale back strikes as ceasefire and disarmament talks remain stalled.

Source: Times of Israel

Nepal-Tibet flood death toll passes 600 as threat from unstable glacial lake eases

The confirmed death toll from last week's Himalayan glacier collapse and flash flood has climbed past 600 in Nepal, plus seven in Tibet, with nearly 3,000 people still missing, including 41 Australians and roughly 900 Nepali hydropower workers feared trapped in tunnels. More than 93,000 people are believed to need aid, according to the Red Cross. Chinese authorities said Saturday the size of the unstable glacial lake formed by the disaster has shrunk, easing fears of a second wave of flooding along the China-Nepal border.

Source: CNN, ABC News (Australia)

Carney says Canada is "at war" with US as trade dispute deepens

Prime Minister Mark Carney escalated his rhetoric on the trade standoff with Washington, saying "you're at war when you get attacked — we got attacked," after talks collapsed and 50% US tariffs took hold on roughly $20 billion of Canadian goods. Carney blamed the breakdown on what he called "uneconomic" and "unfair" US demands and reaffirmed Canada will impose matching, dollar-for-dollar retaliatory tariffs on more than 700 categories of American goods starting September 8.

Source: NPR, NBC News

RSF raids kill civilians and displace hundreds more near besieged El Obeid, Sudan

Rapid Support Forces fighters raided the villages of Um Dareisa and Al-Shaqla southwest of El Obeid, killing at least six civilians and forcing hundreds more to flee toward the besieged North Kordofan capital. Over 5,000 families have arrived in El Obeid in the past week alone, adding to a city already battered by relentless RSF drone strikes on water and power infrastructure, a worsening cholera outbreak, and reports of widespread sexual violence against women and girls.

Source: Sudan Tribune
