September 19, 2026

Trump signs sweeping Russia-Iran sanctions bill into law

President Trump signed the Lindsey O. Graham Sanctioning Russia and Iran Act of 2026, named for the Republican senator who negotiated it before his death in July. The law authorizes sanctions on Russian President Vladimir Putin, senior officials, oligarchs, banks and the energy and defence sectors, along with the "shadow fleet" of tankers used to evade existing sanctions, and gives Trump authority to impose tariffs of up to 100% on the five largest buyers of Russian oil and gas. It passed the Senate 86-11 in August and the House 262-159 on September 16 with rare bipartisan support.

Source: Al Jazeera, RFE/RL

US and Denmark reach deal expanding US military presence in Greenland

Trump announced a deal with Denmark to expand the US military footprint in Greenland while leaving the island under Danish and Greenlandic sovereignty, ending months of threats to take the territory by force. Trump said the agreement grants the US "permanent control over security" with guaranteed access, basing and overflight rights, and that his administration would immediately begin building up a larger military presence on the mineral-rich territory. Denmark said the deal is to be signed next week during the UN General Assembly but still requires approval from the Danish parliament.

Source: Washington Post, NPR

Russia holds parliamentary vote in annexed Ukrainian regions for first time

Russia's parliamentary election, running from Friday through Sunday, for the first time includes residents of the Donetsk, Luhansk, Kherson and Zaporizhzhia regions of Ukraine that Moscow illegally annexed, with Russian authorities citing more than 3.5 million registered voters there. The European Commission called the voting "another blatant violation of international law" and said the EU will never recognize the results, while Ukrainian rights activists reported election workers going house to house escorted by Russian troops and security agents to pressure people to vote.

Source: NPR, NBC News

Trump to meet Gulf leaders at UN General Assembly over next steps in Iran war

Trump is expected to meet the leaders of Saudi Arabia, the UAE, Qatar, Bahrain, Kuwait and Oman on the sidelines of the UN General Assembly in New York next Tuesday to discuss the next phase of the war with Iran. The talks are expected to focus on US ideas for a postwar strategy, with a fuller "day-after" plan reportedly not expected to be finalized until after the November midterms, as Trump has struggled to articulate a clear endgame seven months into the conflict.

Source: Axios

Sweden's Andersson tasked with forming centre-left coalition government

Sweden's parliamentary speaker formally asked Social Democrat leader Magdalena Andersson to form a government after her centre-left bloc won a one-seat majority over Ulf Kristersson's centre-right coalition in the September 15 election. Andersson, who has until September 28 to report back to the speaker, has said she will seek a coalition agreement with the Left, Centre and Green parties, ending four years of centre-right rule that had relied on support from the far-right Sweden Democrats.

Source: Al Jazeera, France 24

Fatal Gaza building collapse underscores fragility of one-year-old ceasefire

A multistory building collapse in Gaza City killed 21 people, the Hamas-run health ministry said, drawing fresh attention to the hazards facing Palestinians nearly a year after an October 2025 ceasefire promised to kickstart reconstruction. Israel continues to bar entry of almost all construction materials and has said it will not permit rebuilding until Hamas disarms, leaving more than 2,000 additional buildings at risk of collapse and over a million people living in damaged or unsafe structures.

Source: Times of Israel, US News (AP)
