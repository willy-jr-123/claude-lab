Houthis seize Red Sea "Gates of Grief" chokepoint as Iran-Gulf Hormuz talks are postponed

Yemen's Houthi rebels captured the Bab el-Mandeb chokepoint known as the "Gates of Grief," giving Iran-aligned forces effective control of two of the world's most important oil shipping lanes while the Strait of Hormuz remains disrupted by the ongoing US-Iran war. A planned Monday meeting in Oman between Iran and Gulf Arab states over a temporary Hormuz shipping corridor was postponed "for now" at Saudi Arabia's request, Iran's foreign ministry said. Separately, Iranian state media reported an Iranian commercial vessel was struck near Qeshm Island in the Strait of Hormuz over the weekend, killing one person and injuring three others.

Source: Bloomberg, CBS News

Trump tells Zelensky to stop striking Russian refineries as diesel shortage worsens

President Trump on Sunday urged Ukraine to halt its campaign of strikes on Russian refineries, saying "he has to stop knocking out diesel fuel in Russia" because the attacks are "causing a shortage" that is "hurting the world." Ukraine has carried out more than 60 strikes on at least two dozen Russian refineries this year to raise the economic cost of the invasion. President Volodymyr Zelensky pushed back, saying that if Russian strikes keep causing blackouts in Ukraine, "we will try to respond to them in kind," defending the refinery strikes as legitimate military targets.

Source: CBS News, The Moscow Times

BRICS leaders wrap up New Delhi summit as bloc tests unity under US pressure

The 18th BRICS summit concluded in New Delhi with leaders adopting the New Delhi Declaration after two days of talks hosted by Prime Minister Narendra Modi. Xi Jinping, Vladimir Putin and Iran's Masoud Pezeshkian held a series of bilateral meetings on the sidelines, as the expanded bloc — now including the UAE, Indonesia, Egypt and Ethiopia alongside founding members — sought common ground on responding to US tariffs, sanctions and the ongoing Iran war, testing how much cohesion the grouping can muster under external pressure.

Source: NPR, CNBC

Sweden's far right loses ground for first time as center-left edges ahead in knife-edge vote

Sweden's Sweden Democrats suffered their first drop in seats and vote share since entering parliament 16 years ago, with support sliding to roughly 17.6% from 20.5% in the last election. Preliminary counts put the center-left bloc led by former Prime Minister Magdalena Andersson's Social Democrats narrowly ahead of Prime Minister Ulf Kristersson's four-party alliance, 176 seats to 173, a result that — if it holds — would likely block the Sweden Democrats from entering government for the first time as had been widely anticipated ahead of the vote.

Source: CBS News, Euronews

Canada explores "associate member" status with EU as it seeks to pivot away from US trade

Prime Minister Mark Carney is exploring a newly conceived "associate member" status with the European Union, the Wall Street Journal reported, as Ottawa looks to reorient trade away from the United States, which currently absorbs about 72% of Canadian exports. Carney said Sunday that Canada is seeking a "unique alliance" with the bloc rather than membership itself, while a government source told CTV News Canada is "not seeking EU membership in any capacity." The report comes ahead of a Carney address to the European Parliament in Strasbourg this week outlining his vision for Canada-EU ties.

Source: CTV News, CBC News

Trump floats US staying in Iran to "keep the oil" as gas prices hit record highs

President Trump said the United States could ultimately remain in Iran and "keep the oil," comparing the idea to Washington's arrangement over Venezuelan oil reserves: "We'll ultimately get out, unless we decide to stay and keep the oil like Venezuela." The remark came as US gasoline prices hit records near $4.30 a gallon amid the ongoing Iran war's supply shock. Trump has said prices are unlikely to fall meaningfully until after November's midterm elections, when he has predicted the conflict will end.

Source: The Epoch Times, CNBC
