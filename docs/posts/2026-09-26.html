September 26, 2026

Trump rejects Iran's seven-day roadmap to reopen Strait of Hormuz

President Trump told reporters "I reject their proposal," dismissing an Iranian plan submitted through mediators at the UN General Assembly that would have reopened the Strait of Hormuz within seven days in exchange for the US lifting its naval blockade of Iranian ports, waiving oil sanctions and observing a ceasefire including Lebanon. The Wall Street Journal reported Trump has told aides he views renewed US bombing of Iran after the midterm elections as likely.

Source: Al Jazeera, Washington Post

Zelensky says Trump made "final decision" to license Ukraine to produce Patriot missiles

Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky said Trump told him during their UN General Assembly sidelines meeting in New York that Washington has decided to grant Kyiv a license to manufacture Patriot interceptor missiles domestically. The move would address a persistent shortage as Russia escalates drone and missile barrages, reversing months of US hesitation over transferring the sensitive production technology.

Source: CNN, Euronews

Russian drones hit Kyiv overnight as EU releases €6.6 billion in stalled military aid

Russia struck four districts of Kyiv overnight with a barrage that included 173 Shahed-type drones, damaging residential buildings and injuring at least five people, Mayor Vitali Klitschko said. Separately, EU member states agreed to release €6.6 billion in European Peace Facility funding for Ukraine that had been stalled since 2023 over a Hungarian veto, with €900 million earmarked for the bloc's military training mission and €4.7 billion to reimburse member states' supplies.

Source: Kyiv Independent, Euronews

Qatari PM calls Netanyahu "biggest obstacle" to Gaza peace plan

Qatari Prime Minister Mohammed bin Abdulrahman Al-Thani told CNN that Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu is "the biggest obstacle right now" to advancing the Gaza peace plan, accusing his government of a "long list of violations" for the sake of political gain. Al-Thani said Israel controls more than 70% of Gaza's territory despite the ceasefire and that humanitarian aid deliveries remain far below the agreed daily quota.

Source: Times of Israel, Jerusalem Post

Pakistan locks down Islamabad as PTI postpones long march to October 4

Pakistani authorities sealed roads into the capital with roughly 2,000 shipping containers and deployed nearly 30,000 security personnel to block a planned march by supporters of jailed former Prime Minister Imran Khan demanding his release. Khyber Pakhtunkhwa's chief minister announced the Pakistan Tehreek-e-Insaf march, originally set for September 27, would instead begin from Peshawar on October 4, after the lockdown reportedly cost about $168,000 a day in container rental alone.

Source: Dawn, Bloomberg

Venezuela's Rodriguez leaves Washington without a deal as Machado vows return

Venezuela's interim president Delcy Rodriguez departed the US after UN General Assembly meetings without securing an agreement with Washington or a date for new elections, having sought support on debt, energy and mining following Nicolas Maduro's removal by US forces in January. Opposition leader Maria Corina Machado, who has been blocked from re-entering Venezuela at least three times in recent weeks, said she still plans to return home before the end of 2026 and run for president again.

Source: Modern Diplomacy, The Hill
