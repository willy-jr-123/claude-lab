August 24, 2026

Trump raises Canadian auto tariffs to 50% as trade war deepens

President Trump said the United States will raise tariffs on Canadian cars, trucks and auto parts to 50%, effectively doubling the current 25% rate, after last-ditch talks between Washington and Ottawa collapsed over the weekend. The move comes days after a separate 50% US tariff on roughly $20 billion of other Canadian goods took effect. Prime Minister Mark Carney said Washington had "asked too much" and confirmed Canada will impose matching retaliatory tariffs on US steel, dairy, appliances, farm equipment, pulp, paper and electronics starting September 8, with the Canadian dollar sliding on the news.

Source: CNBC, Bloomberg

US unveils "economic D-Day" sanctions on Iran as Tehran threatens Hormuz seizures

Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent said Washington aims to "sever every economic lifeline" sustaining Iran, unveiling what officials billed as the most sweeping sanctions package yet against Tehran's oil exports and financial networks. Iran's top security official, Mohsen Rezaei, warned Tehran would "retaliate in a seismic manner," treat any country supporting the sanctions as committing "an act of war," and threatened to seize ships in the Strait of Hormuz. Traffic through the strait remains thin, though Iran has allowed several Iraqi oil tankers to pass.

Source: CNN, CNBC

Israel threatens to "intensify" Gaza strikes over children's kites, kills several in new attacks

Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu and Defense Minister Israel Katz warned Hamas that the military would intensify strikes and order new evacuations in response to kites flown from Gaza toward Israeli border communities, which Palestinian officials say were made of paper by children and posed no threat. Overnight, Israeli strikes hit a mosque and a water desalination plant in Gaza City's Sheikh Radwan neighborhood, and a separate strike on a displacement tent near Al-Aqsa Hospital killed two brothers. The Gaza Health Ministry and Israeli authorities put the war's combined death toll above 75,000.

Source: Al Jazeera, Times of Israel

Russia hits Odesa hypermarkets and warehouses as Ukraine strikes Russian airbase

Russian missiles and drones struck Odesa region overnight, setting two Epicentr home-improvement hypermarkets ablaze and hitting food storage and energy infrastructure, with regional officials reporting several people injured. Russia's defense ministry said it also targeted a fuel tanker and military-linked warehouses at the Yuzhne port. In return, Ukrainian forces struck a Russian airbase, part of an intensifying exchange that has included Kyiv's growing use of long-range Flamingo missiles against targets deep inside Russia.

Source: Meduza, Ukrainska Pravda

Chinese vessel activity near Taiwan hits record for third straight month

Taiwan's defense ministry said it detected Chinese coast guard, survey and other official vessels near the island 118 times in the first 24 days of August, already surpassing July's record of 117 and June's 111 — the highest monthly totals since Taiwan began publishing the data in 2024. Analysts at the Asia Maritime Transparency Initiative described a "more sustained and assertive" Chinese law-enforcement presence in waters east of Taiwan, adding to concerns in Taipei and allied capitals that Beijing may be rehearsing a maritime containment strategy around the island.

Source: South China Morning Post

Sudanese army drones strike RSF positions in Nyala

Suspected Sudanese army drones struck several Rapid Support Forces positions in Nyala, South Darfur, early Monday, triggering heavy explosions near the city's airport, in the al-Riyadh neighborhood and around the University of Nyala campus. The strikes came hours after a large cargo aircraft landed in the RSF-held city. More than three years into Sudan's civil war, the conflict has displaced millions and left over 8 million children out of school, with humanitarian officials continuing to call it the world's largest displacement crisis.

Source: Sudan Tribune
