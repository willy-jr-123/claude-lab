September 20, 2026

Ukraine launches its largest-ever drone attack on Moscow, hitting oil refinery on final day of Russian election

Russia's defence ministry said it downed more than 1,100 Ukrainian drones overnight across 19 regions, the largest single-night total reported this year, with Moscow Mayor Sergei Sobyanin saying roughly 450 of them were aimed at the capital. The strikes damaged the Gazpromneft-Moscow refinery in the Kapotnya district, sparking a large fire at several processing units, and killed two people in the Moscow region. Sobyanin said the attack was "clearly planned" to coincide with the final day of Russia's parliamentary election, including the disputed vote held in annexed Ukrainian territory.

Source: NPR, Al Jazeera

Houthi strikes hit near Riyadh's main airport and a Saudi Aramco facility

Yemen's Houthi movement claimed missile and drone strikes on "sensitive sites" in Riyadh after flames and a large plume of black smoke were seen near King Khalid International Airport, with flight-tracking data showing major disruption there. The Houthis said they separately struck an Aramco facility in the Red Sea port city of Yanbu, a key oil export hub. Saudi authorities had issued air-raid alerts beforehand but had not confirmed casualties or the extent of damage, in the latest escalation of Houthi attacks on the kingdom tied to the wider regional war.

Source: Washington Post, Euronews

China threatens to scrap Trump-Xi summit over Taiwan arms sale, as Trump weighs delay

Beijing has warned it may cancel the planned September 24 summit between Xi Jinping and Donald Trump if Washington proceeds with a $14 billion Taiwan arms package that Congress authorized in January but Trump has not yet signed. Chinese officials have called Taiwan a "red line" in the relationship, and Trump is reportedly weighing pushing a decision on the sale to later in the year to preserve the summit, a delicate balancing act with midterm elections roughly ten weeks away.

Source: South China Morning Post, Taipei Times

US, Denmark and Greenland set to sign military-basing deal during UN General Assembly week

The office of Danish Prime Minister Mette Frederiksen said the security agreement expanding the US military footprint in Greenland, announced by Trump on September 18, is expected to be formally signed by the three governments this week on the sidelines of the UN General Assembly in New York. Frederiksen said the deal preserves Greenlandic and Danish sovereignty and self-determination, though it still requires approval from the Danish parliament before taking effect.

Source: NPR, France 24

World leaders converge on New York as UN General Assembly's high-level week opens

The UN General Assembly's 81st session enters its High-Level Week on September 22, drawing heads of state and government to New York amid the ongoing Iran war, the Russia-Ukraine conflict, and fragile ceasefires in Gaza and Lebanon. Trump is expected to address the assembly along with dozens of other leaders, with sideline meetings planned on Gulf states' role in a postwar Iran strategy and the Greenland security agreement, in what US officials have described as a consequential year for the institution.

Source: Washington Times, US Department of State

Israeli settler shot dead at West Bank spring; IDF sweep detains dozens of Palestinians

Netanel Shakron, a 36-year-old father of six, was shot and killed at a spring in the central West Bank where he had gone to bathe with his son ahead of Yom Kippur, according to Israeli officials. The Israeli military said it arrested a suspect who had fled to a nearby hospital. Separately, Israeli troops carried out a large counterterrorism operation in the northern West Bank town of al-Mughayyir, with Palestinian officials saying at least 28 people were detained and vehicles seized.

Source: Times of Israel, AP
