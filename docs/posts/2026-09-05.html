September 5, 2026

Witkoff and Kushner arrive in Moscow with plan to "end the war," head to Kyiv next

US special envoys Steve Witkoff and Jared Kushner landed in Moscow on Saturday, where they were met by Russian presidential envoy Kirill Dmitriev, ahead of a meeting with President Vladimir Putin and a subsequent trip to Kyiv to see President Volodymyr Zelensky. President Donald Trump said the pair are carrying a "proposal to end the war" and blamed the diplomatic deadlock partly on personal animosity between Zelensky and Putin. Putin ordered a three-day pause in strikes on Kyiv to coincide with the envoys' visit, but a wide gap reportedly remains between Moscow and Kyiv on core terms as the war heads into its fifth year.

Source: CNBC, Euronews

Trump calls Iran conflict "small potatoes," backs Vance's line that it isn't a "war"

Speaking in the Oval Office on Friday, President Trump described the months-long conflict with Iran as "small potatoes," saying "it's not a big thing," and defended Vice President JD Vance's earlier assertion that the fighting does not amount to a war. The remarks came despite the deaths of 18 US service members in the conflict and a spike in global energy prices, and follow criticism that the administration bypassed Congress before launching strikes on Iran. Separately, the US military has warned Iran's Revolutionary Guard that any attack on US-linked vessels will draw a disproportionate response against Iran's oil fleet.

Source: Al Jazeera, The Washington Post

US ambassador denies Gaza expulsion plan, demands "severe consequences" for West Bank settler violence

US Ambassador to Israel Mike Huckabee said Saturday there is "categorically" no active Israeli plan to force Palestinians out of Gaza, days after far-right National Security Minister Itamar Ben Gvir outlined a proposal to remove 250,000 Gazans in the first year and the rest of the territory's roughly two million residents over the following six, and Defense Minister Israel Katz said Israel was ready to remove Palestinians by sea and land pending US approval. Visiting the West Bank, Huckabee also called for "severe consequences" to end settler violence against Palestinians, after an Israeli broadcaster reported a 63% surge in "nationalistic crime" by extremist settlers in the first half of 2026.

Source: Al Jazeera, Dawn

More than 60 killed as Yemen's government forces and Houthi rebels clash near Bab al-Mandab

Fighting between Yemen's Saudi-backed government forces and Iran-backed Houthi rebels killed more than 60 people on Saturday, including 26 government troops and 31 Houthi fighters according to military and medical sources, in a battle for a gateway to the Red Sea. The clashes follow some of the deadliest fighting in years a day earlier and come after the collapse in July of a 2022 ceasefire, with government forces mounting counterattacks against a Houthi ground offensive aimed at cutting off the port city of Mokha and advancing toward the Bab al-Mandab Strait.

Source: Dawn

Philippines Vice President Sara Duterte posts bail after arrest order over alleged Marcos assassination threat

Philippines Vice President Sara Duterte posted bail Saturday after a court issued an order for her arrest a day earlier on charges she publicly threatened to have President Ferdinand Marcos Jr., his wife, and then-House Speaker Martin Romualdez assassinated. The charges stem from a 2024 online news conference in which Duterte warned she would arrange for the trio to be killed if she herself were killed amid escalating political disputes; she has denied threatening Marcos and says she was expressing concern for her own safety. The case adds to Duterte's legal troubles months after the Marcos-allied House voted to impeach her over separate allegations of unexplained wealth and misuse of confidential funds.

Source: The Washington Post, NPR

Canada's retaliatory tariffs on roughly $28 billion of US goods set to take effect September 8

Canada is set to impose "dollar-for-dollar" retaliatory tariffs of 15%, 25% and 50% on about $28 billion of US goods starting September 8, targeting steel, aluminum, dairy, appliances, agricultural equipment, pulp and paper, plastics and electronics, among other sectors. The countermeasures respond to US tariffs on Canadian goods that took effect August 22 after trade talks between Ottawa and Washington collapsed, and mark a further escalation in a trade dispute Prime Minister Mark Carney has said Canada will match "dollar for dollar" to protect its workers and businesses.

Source: Canada.ca, Al Jazeera
