US destroys five Iranian oil tankers as Iran retaliates with missile strikes on US base in Jordan

US forces destroyed five Iranian crude tankers — the Kivik, Charminar, Horizon 1, Riesco and Derya — on Tuesday after Iran's Revolutionary Guard fired ballistic missiles at a US Navy warship for the second time in two days; crews were warned to abandon ship before the strikes. Iran retaliated by launching around 20 ballistic missiles at a US-linked base near Al Azraq in Jordan, but Jordanian air defenses intercepted 18 of them, with the remainder falling in unpopulated areas and no casualties reported. US forces have now destroyed ten Iranian tankers in the past week alone as the seven-month-old conflict continues to escalate.

Source: Al Jazeera, NPR

Russian strikes kill at least 12 and injure dozens across Ukraine as Kyiv is hit again

Russian attacks killed at least 12 people and injured 66 others across Ukraine over the past day, with Kyiv hit repeatedly overnight. A Russian drone struck a 24-story residential building in the capital's Darnytskyi district, injuring five people and forcing the evacuation of 23 residents, while a separate drone strike on a nine-story building in the Dniprovskyi district killed a woman and injured another resident. The renewed barrage came days after a brief pause in strikes on the capital coinciding with visits by US envoys Steve Witkoff and Jared Kushner to Kyiv and Moscow.

Source: The Kyiv Independent, Meduza

UK, France, Canada and other allies move to ban trade with Israeli West Bank settlements

Britain, France and Canada announced coordinated plans to restrict trade in goods produced in Israeli settlements in the occupied West Bank, with UK officials accusing settlers of conducting what they termed "ethnic cleansing." Denmark, Finland, Iceland, Ireland, Norway, Poland, Portugal, Spain and Sweden also signaled support for national or EU-wide restrictions, in a move widely seen as underscoring Israel's growing isolation. US and Israeli officials sharply criticized the measures, with Israeli ministers accusing the countries of interfering in Israeli politics ahead of elections.

Source: CNN, Bloomberg

UN warns Houthi attacks on Saudi Arabia risk sparking full-scale regional war

UN Special Envoy for Yemen Hans Grundberg called on the Iran-backed Houthis to halt missile and drone attacks on Saudi Arabia, warning that strikes on civilian and energy infrastructure risk widening the conflict and undermining prospects for a political solution. The warning follows Tuesday's Houthi attacks that wounded more than 70 people in Saudi cities including Abha, Jazan and Khamis Mushait, which the group said were retaliation for Saudi-backed strikes on a Yemeni prison. A years-long truce in Yemen's civil war effectively collapsed last month as fighting between the Houthis and Saudi-backed government forces intensified.

Source: The National, NPR

Oil prices top $100 a barrel for first time since July as Middle East conflicts intensify

Brent crude climbed above $100 a barrel on Wednesday, its highest level since July, after the US strikes on Iranian tankers, Iran's retaliatory missile attack on Jordan, and the Houthi assault on Saudi Arabia stoked fresh fears over regional energy supply. Analysts at Goldman Sachs said the seven-month-old US-Iran conflict is raising the risk of prices surging past $120 a barrel if attacks on shipping and infrastructure continue to intensify.

Source: CNBC, CNN Business

CIA Director Ratcliffe to take bigger role in stalled Russia-Ukraine peace talks

CIA Director John Ratcliffe is preparing to take on a larger role in US negotiations with Russia and Ukraine, according to officials who briefed European counterparts, as the White House considers scaling back the involvement of special envoy Steve Witkoff and Jared Kushner. The shift follows Ratcliffe's previously undisclosed trip to Moscow, where he met Russian intelligence chiefs Sergey Naryshkin and Alexander Bortnikov and reportedly floated the idea of a Trump-Putin-Zelensky summit, in the clearest sign yet that Washington is searching for new channels to jump-start the moribund four-and-a-half-year-old peace process.

Source: Axios, CNN
