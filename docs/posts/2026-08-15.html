August 15, 2026

Trump says he will declare the Strait of Hormuz a "US territory" once Iran war ends

President Donald Trump told a crowd in New York that he plans to declare the Strait of Hormuz a U.S. "territory" once the war with Iran concludes, saying the U.S. naval blockade means "no ships get through unless we want them to." Trump did not explain how such a declaration would work legally, since the strait falls under the joint jurisdiction of Iran and Oman rather than the United States. Iran's deputy foreign minister, Kazem Gharibabadi, dismissed the remarks on social media, reiterating Tehran's rejection of what it calls an illegal blockade.

Source: Al Jazeera, The Hill

Ukraine strikes Russian airbase and Roscosmos rocket facility deep inside Russia

Ukraine said it used domestically produced Flamingo cruise missiles to strike the Savasleyka military airbase, about 700 kilometers from the border, along with the Progress Rocket Space Center in Samara, a key Roscosmos facility. President Volodymyr Zelensky said the airbase had hosted MiG-31K jets capable of carrying the Kinzhal ballistic missiles used against Ukrainian cities. The strikes are part of Kyiv's ongoing campaign against military-industrial targets deep inside Russia as the war nears its fifth year with no ceasefire in place.

Source: The Kyiv Independent

Canada rejects latest US tariff offer days before 50% duties are set to take effect

Canadian officials rejected Washington's latest counteroffer in trade talks, sources told CBC News, with only days left before the U.S. is due to impose 50% tariffs on roughly $20 billion of Canadian goods on August 19. Trade Minister Dominic LeBlanc met U.S. Trade Representative Jamieson Greer in Washington this week for the two sides' third round of talks in as many weeks, covering Canada's retaliatory auto tariffs, provincial alcohol restrictions and dairy quotas. A Canadian government source said Washington also wants a deal before the deadline, though Ottawa remains unsatisfied with the terms on offer.

Source: CBC News, U.S. News & World Report (Reuters)

Sudan's army chief says he's open to peace as fighting rages near El Obeid

Sudanese Sovereignty Council chief Abdel Fattah al-Burhan said the army remains committed to defeating the Rapid Support Forces but is open to "genuine" peace initiatives, while ruling out any RSF role in governing Sudan going forward. The statement came as heavy fighting broke out southwest of El Obeid, the North Kordofan state capital, where the RSF launched a dawn assault on army positions that was repelled with artillery and drone strikes. The RSF has also been mobilizing tribal recruits in Darfur following recent battlefield setbacks and a wave of commander defections.

Source: Anadolu Agency

Israeli strikes kill Gaza police chief and others as fragile truce frays further

An Israeli drone strike killed Gaza City police director Col. Jamal Abu Kamil, 43, when missiles hit his vehicle on al-Rashid Street; Israel's military said it targeted him as a Hamas operative, a claim Gaza's Interior Ministry disputed, calling it an assassination. A separate strike in Khan Younis killed a person the Israeli military described as a Hamas commander, part of a broader wave of strikes this week that has killed several Palestinians despite the nominal ceasefire in place since October. The killings followed the end of a weeklong pause in Israeli air raids on the strip.

Source: Al Jazeera

Yemen edges closer to full-scale war as Houthi-government clashes intensify in Taiz

Yemeni government forces and Houthi fighters have traded escalating attacks around Taiz province for a third straight day, with more than 27 Houthi fighters reported killed and 19 wounded after government troops repelled an assault on the Al-Barh front using artillery, mortars and drones. The clashes followed a Houthi missile and drone attack near the strategic port of Mokha, which prompted government shelling of Houthi positions across several fronts in the province. The UN's special envoy has warned that Yemen faces its highest risk of returning to full-scale war since a 2022 truce took hold.

Source: Middle East Monitor
